Nor would adding wake/availability losses by itself explain modeled energy already below observed net production. Keep possible terrain, turbine and reporting explanations separate until data establish their contributions.

## Processing checks before the full production run

1. **Fix study meaning and geography.** **Fixed reference layout confirmed by the author November 24**, with historical matched fleets for validation. This isolates weather effects from deployment change; the result is a reference-system resource assessment, not historical production. The [protocol](study_protocol.md) explains the rationale. Proposed reference year is 2020; final wind/PV technology choices, complete PV weights and inventory hashes still need to be frozen after physical validation. Rated-capacity shares and scope remain explicit scenarios.
2. **Define the study calendar and input support.** A proposed UTC core interval is `[2005-01-01 00:00, 2025-01-01 00:00)`, totaling **175,320 hours**, including five leap days. Its hourly interval-end labels run from January 1, 2005 at 01:00 to January 1, 2025 at 00:00 UTC. Obtain bracketing instantaneous samples and accumulated radiation at the required end labels, including the final boundary timestamp. Include explicit window/event context outside the core and retain any remaining censoring flags. Pacific-month validation is a separate support convention, not a silent shift of the climate record.
3. **Prove chunk continuity.** The [January verification](chunk_continuity.md) passes two/six-file partitions, 18 window cases and synthetic calendar boundaries. A [new limited real-weather rehearsal](pre_expansion_checks.md) passes January–February 2020 monthly partitions, including February 29 and the final endpoint. It also checks July 2020/January 2021. Hourly/window catalogues match continuous processing. Verified cached replay performs zero downloads. Full-grid direct-CDS source consistency and production restart/performance still require rehearsal. January-only observation importers remain restricted; aware DST conversion was tested, ambiguous naive local labels rejected.
4. **Audit every input batch.** [Implemented preflight](../scripts/archive_preflight.py) checks grid, complete timestamps, variables/units/product semantics, explicit final ERA5 expver, vector flags and finite/plausible values; the supplied pilot passes. [242 calendar-valid monthly requests](../results/tables/era5_2005_2024_request_plan.json) are generated **but not submitted**. Receipt tests reject changed requests, partial/missing receipts and tampering. Archive schema/extraction and a production scheduler still need a limited direct-source rehearsal. Raw data and credentials remain outside Git.
5. **Predefine event analysis.** [Protocol recorded](study_protocol.md): retain strict hourly 20%/24h and 48h combined 6% reference analyses, the existing share/threshold grid, whole-event versus core exposure, censoring and threshold-deficit interpretation. No threshold was changed to obtain events. Final production physical configuration is not yet validated; window support is not continuously low-hour duration.
6. **Define temperature comparisons.** [Tested helpers](../scripts/temperature_extremes.py) implement complete UTC daily means, leave-one-year-out seasonal 5/95% study-sample quantiles, leap-day interpolation and sustained three-day runs. The protocol distinguishes statewide background temperature from local extrema/demand. Real climatology, renewable/temperature overlap and uncertainty estimators await final inputs and implementation; no pilot-derived extreme thresholds are claimed.
7. **Run a multi-month rehearsal.** Confirm reproducibility, output metadata, memory/storage needs and resumable acquisition on a small continuous range. Then process the larger archive using the tested pipeline and review QC summaries before making frequency/severity claims. Sparse extreme events and autocorrelation require explicit uncertainty treatment in the final study.

No additional access was needed for the fixed-tilt comparison or public-weather rehearsal. A configured direct-CDS client and independent wind/turbine reference information are still needed for the remaining direct-source and physical-validation gates. Scope retrieval to the selected validation periods first. Copernicus credentials belong in the configured private client environment, never in chat or Git. Do not expand simply to mask unresolved amplitude errors with a longer record.
